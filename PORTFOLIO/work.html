  • Finance Athulitha Laboratories Finance & Operations Intern · May–July 2026
    The company
    Athulitha Laboratories Pvt. Ltd. is a pharmaceutical manufacturer specialising in active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) for the veterinary segment.
    My part
    Built a three-year Excel financial analysis model (FY22–FY25), using common-size analysis, year-on-year growth and CAGR to evaluate the company's cost structure, profitability and financial performance.   • Tech HR attrition prediction Full-Stack Machine Learning Application

  • Entrepreneurship ₹0 Venture Challenge — Crochet Pop-up Stall Entrepreneurship Challenge
    The opportunity
    As part of a two-hour, zero-investment venture challenge, I identified an opportunity to help a friend sell her unsold handmade crochet products. She had leftover inventory from a college stall, which I saw as an opportunity to create a small resale venture without investing in product development or inventory.
    My Approach
    I arranged to take the products on a consignment basis, retaining my friend's agreed price and adding a small margin to each item. I set up a pop-up stall in my residential community between 6:30 PM and 8:30 PM, targeting the evening period with higher footfall. I handled the stall setup, pricing, customer interactions and sales, then settled the agreed amount with the original maker.
    Results
    ₹0 initial investment. ₹4,625 in total sales. ₹825 profit. Nearly 20 pieces sold within two hours.
    Skills
    Opportunity Identification · Zero-Capital Business Models · Consignment-Based Selling · Pricing Strategy · Inventory Utilisation · Customer Engagement
